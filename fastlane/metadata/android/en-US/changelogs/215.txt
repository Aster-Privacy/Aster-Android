Bug fixes and improvements:

- Aster Mail responds faster and stops unexpectedly less often.
- Sign-up takes four pages, and you can choose a plan as you sign up.
- You get a warning when a sender's name imitates a brand or hides another address.
- With a filter on, the inbox loads every matching message.
- PGP and plain-text patch messages show their full body.
- Muted categories stay silent, and notifications vibrate again.
