Bug fixes and improvements:

- When someone replies to you, their earlier message is tucked behind a collapsed quote again instead of running on below your reply
- Drafts save in place as you write and clear once the message goes out, so you no longer end up with duplicates
- Attachment details open correctly on encrypted messages, and an attachment you cannot open yet is now labeled as locked
- Buttons in the message reader are larger and easier to hit, and a tap no longer falls through to the message underneath
- Encryption key publishing is more reliable, so messages to other Aster users are less likely to stall
