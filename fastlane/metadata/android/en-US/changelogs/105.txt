Bug fixes and improvements:

- Fixed a crash that could close the app when creating an alias or opening the Aliases screen.
- Fixed some opened emails not being marked as read, especially when you opened and quickly left a message.
- Fixed the inbox briefly showing already-deleted or archived emails when you opened the app.
- Improved push notification reliability after the app has been closed for a long time.
- Fixed password-protected messages: the password box no longer autocapitalizes or autocorrects, so the password always matches when the recipient opens it.
