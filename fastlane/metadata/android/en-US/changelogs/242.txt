Bug fixes and improvements:

- Aster Mail opens faster, and your inbox no longer pauses after launch.
- New-mail notifications arrive reliably and show verification codes again.
- Messages open right away with the subject and sender in place.
- Long conversations open at the newest message instead of partway through.
- Scrolling your inbox, threads, and contacts is smoother.
- Search opens cleanly, and the selection count no longer flickers as you drag.
