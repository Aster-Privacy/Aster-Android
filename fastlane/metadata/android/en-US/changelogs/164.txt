Bug fixes and improvements:

- You can now turn sender pictures on or off in settings, under Behavior. It syncs to your other devices.
- Approve a new desktop or Bridge device from your phone by entering the code that device shows.
- Every tap registers when you select messages in your inbox.
- Replies to plain text messages collapse the quoted conversation instead of repeating it all.
- Screen readers read message rows correctly, and formatted messages display more reliably.
