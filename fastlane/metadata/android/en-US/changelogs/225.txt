Bug fixes and improvements:

- Reorder or hide sidebar folders, and the change syncs to your other devices.
- Contacts load offline, and edits you make offline sync when you reconnect.
- Delete messages forever from Trash.
- Lock folders so their mail needs your password to open.
- Birthdays can skip the year, and you can choose the first day of the week.
- Reactions are encrypted like replies.
- Accounts missing an encryption key get one at sign-in.
