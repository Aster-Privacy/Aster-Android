- The inbox opens straight into your messages instead of a blank loading state

- A cleaner contact page, search filters, and export screen
- Creating an SMTP token no longer flickers
- Unread only no longer loads forever or snaps back when you scroll
- Sign up continues after you paste a password
- Order details no longer appear inside a plain reply
