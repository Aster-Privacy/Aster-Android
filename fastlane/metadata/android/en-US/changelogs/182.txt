Bug fixes and improvements:

- Your inbox no longer reloads when the app renews your sign-in
- Printing a message works in every app language
- Replies you send show your own text and no longer stay in Drafts
- If the server refuses a message, you see why and it stops retrying
- Sent messages no longer fail when the Sent copy lacks attachments
- Aliases shows recently deleted and matching addresses, and loads fully
- Searching for mail from a sender shows only that exact address
