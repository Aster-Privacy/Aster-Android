Bug fixes and improvements:

- A reply you send shows your own text in Sent and in the message list
- Sent messages no longer stay in Drafts
- When the server refuses a message, you see the reason and it stops retrying
- A sent message no longer reports a failure when its Sent copy is missing attachments
- Recently deleted aliases open on their own screen, and your matching addresses show on the Aliases screen
- The Aliases screen no longer stays loading when you have no aliases
