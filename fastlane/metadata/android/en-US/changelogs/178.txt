New in this version:

- Conversations read as separate cards again, matching your mail list.
- Turn on a constant connection to get new mail instantly without Google services.
- Long-press the sender or reply-to address in message details to copy it.

Fixed:

- Low network mode stays off unless you turn it on.
- A queued draft can no longer be sent twice, and folder counts update after a send.
- Quiet hours no longer keep resending, and recovery codes offer a retry if they fail to load.
